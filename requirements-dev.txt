# The linter is the one entry here pinned EXACTLY rather than to a floor, and
# the asymmetry is deliberate. A floor lets an upstream release add a rule and
# turn a commit-free Tuesday red: "someone released ruff on Tuesday" becomes
# "main is red on Tuesday". Two of those and the lint step acquires a
# `continue-on-error`, which is how a gate stops being one. The measured
# baseline is ZERO findings at 0.16.1 (2026-08-05) against the standard declared
# in ruff.toml; the exact pin is what keeps "zero findings" meaning the same
# thing next week. Upgrading then costs a deliberate commit with a visible diff
# — which is the right place to argue about a new rule, rather than a red main.
# CI installs this file, so the linter a developer runs locally and the one the
# gate runs are the same by construction. SPEC-CI-001 T1.
ruff==0.16.1
